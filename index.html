About Us

Built by Recruiters,
for Everyone

TalentFlow was founded in 2019 by a team of former recruiters and engineers who were frustrated by how broken the hiring process had become. Endless back-and-forth emails, outdated applicant tracking systems, and gut-feel decisions were costing companies time and money — and losing great candidates to competitors.

We set out to build something fundamentally different: a platform that uses data and intelligent matching to connect the right people with the right companies — faster, fairer, and with far less friction. Today, TalentFlow helps over 3,500 companies across 40 countries hire smarter.

Our team of 150+ is spread across five continents, and we're proud to be a company that practices what it preaches — hiring for skills, potential, and cultural alignment rather than traditional credentials alone.
